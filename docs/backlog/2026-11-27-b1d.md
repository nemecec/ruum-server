# Backlog 2026-11-27 (card B-1d: database bindings of web apps)

What card B-1d decided provisionally and left open (`ruum-server-wt/panel/impl-b1d.md`). IDs
`B1D-Dn` (provisional decisions, design 18.9) and `B1D-n` (open items); the integrator may
renumber them. Kinds, efforts and handlings are as in `docs/backlog/2026-11-08.md`.

Closed by this card: D5-3 (the cluster set-up grants `SET` on `temp_file_limit`), D5-8 (the
binding repair calls `AppRoles::repair_node`; a database in the trash fails like a missing
node), D5-9 (the memory app role store drops the rows of a purged database), S-87 (the tenant
cap holds across replicas: an advisory lock of the platform database), and the B-1d part of
L1-10 (`RUUM_BINDING_<NAME>` of a database: `AppService::binding_env_for_start`). D5-1 and
D5-10 have the provisional answers B1D-D3 and B1D-D2.
