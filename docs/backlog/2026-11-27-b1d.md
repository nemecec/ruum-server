# Backlog 2026-11-27 (card B-1d: database bindings of web apps)

What card B-1d decided provisionally and left open (`ruum-server-wt/panel/impl-b1d.md`). IDs
`B1D-Dn` (provisional decisions, design 18.9) and `B1D-n` (open items); the integrator may
renumber them. Kinds, efforts and handlings are as in `docs/backlog/2026-11-08.md`.

Closed by this card: D5-3 (the cluster set-up grants `SET` on `temp_file_limit`), D5-8 (the
binding repair calls `AppRoles::repair_node`; a database in the trash fails like a missing
node), D5-9 (the memory app role store drops the rows of a purged database), S-87 (the tenant

L1-10 (`RUUM_BINDING_<NAME>` of a database: `AppService::binding_env_for_start`). D5-1 and
D5-10 have the provisional answers B1D-D3 and B1D-D2.

| B1D-D1 | decision (provisional) | The masked branch of a preview is made with the deployer's right (the rules of `createDatabaseBranch`: `edit` on the source, the guest policy, an agent's scope). A deployer with `view` only passes the check of a `view` binding before the `202`, and the stage `bindings` then fails with `403 binding_not_allowed`. | B1D-D2 | decision (provisional) | D5-10: the app roles of one tenant hold at most a share of the cluster's app budget (default `budget / 10`, `PostgresConfig::app_tenant_share`, `RUUM_DATA_APP_TENANT_SHARE`), checked in the engine under the budget lock with the tenant of the database's `ruum_meta.ident`. The engine finds a tenant's roles by a role comment `ruum: app of <tag>`, the tag being 16 hex characters of SHA-256 of the tenant ID. Residual: role comments are readable by every login role of the cluster, so an app learns which (anonymous) roles belong to one tenant and how many; this extends D5-5. Roles made before this card have no tag and do not count against a share (the cluster budget still holds). **Arithmetic (review of B-1d):** an app role has 5 connections; the card's own flow needs 3 roles per database binding at once (the live role, the preview being superseded, the new preview: the old preview's role ends at the end of the new run), and the data cluster test's two bindings need 4. The default share is `max_connections / 20`: dev (`max_connections=200`) gives 10 = **2 roles**; staging (CNPG `max_connections: 100`) gives 5 = **1 role**; a cluster with 1 000 gives 50 = 10 roles. So the laptop gets `RUUM_DATA_APP_TENANT_SHARE=50` from `scripts/dev.sh env` (10 roles; test `the_dev_share_fits_a_publish_and_two_draft_previews` shows the default refuses the flow and 50 fits), and staging needs a setting too (B1D-10). The core cap `MAX_APP_ROLES_PER_TENANT` (20 roles = 100 connections) is reached only with a share of 100 or more. | `postgres/app_roles.rs` `tenant_tag`, `make_cluster_role`; test `a_tenant_holds_at_most_its_share_of_the_budget` | S | **panel**: pick the production default knowingly (keep `budget / 10` with a setting per cluster, or a floor such as `min(budget, MAX_APP_ROLES_PER_TENANT × limit)`) and confirm the residual (a private table in the `postgres` database would hide the tag but needs a set-up step; an HMAC of the tenant ID with a cluster secret instead of the plain SHA-256 would stop an app that knows a tenant ID from counting that tenant's roles). |

| B1D-2 | gap | The choice of a reused branch (the preview of the same draft) and the trash of an unused branch are serialized per process only (`DatabaseBindings::branch_lock`).
