# Backlog 2026-11-12 (product ideas of the person: app sharing and billing)

From the person's ideas on 2026-11-12, written into the design as sections 9.13 and 21.7
(decisions 43 and 44, both provisional). Kinds, efforts and handlings are as in
`docs/backlog/2026-11-08.md`. All decisions here are `provisional` (design 18.9).

## 1. Sharing apps between tenants (design 9.13)

| ID | Kind | Item | Evidence | Effort | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-41 | feature | App packages: publish an immutable version of a project (code, manifests and migrations, roles, bindings, extension points, changelog); channels `stable` and `beta`; withdraw a version; security push to a channel. | design 9.13 | L | **panel** (architect, security) for the package format and the contract, then **pipeline**. |
| P-42 | feature | Install into another tenant: its own app node, database, files and bindings; automatic or reviewed updates; updates run migrations under the schema rule (12.7); go back one version. | design 9.13; decision 42 | L | **pipeline** after P-41. First case: the TajuRuum work management app (step 3) installed by a second office. |
| P-43 | feature | Extension points: extra fields and tables in the tenant's own schema, own views and reports, automations on app events, role mapping, translations and theme, named hooks run with the tenant's rights; an update that breaks a customisation is shown before it runs. | design 9.13 | L | **panel** (architect, UX designer), then **pipeline**. |
| P-44 | feature | Access and licences for packages: private to named tenants, the tenant's catalogue, later a public gallery with review; free or paid (P-47). | design 9.13 | M | **brief** when P-42 works. |

## 2. Billing (design 21.7)

| ID | Kind | Item | Evidence | Effort | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-45 | feature | Level 1, Ruum bills its tenants: plans and usage (21.4), invoices with EU VAT (reverse charge, OSS), card and SEPA through Stripe Billing, receipts as files, dunning (reminders, read-only, no deletion without notice). | P-48 | feature | Level 3, a tenant bills its own customers through Ruum (subscriptions, invoices, payment links; the tenant is the seller with its own Stripe Connect account and VAT). | design 21.7 | L | **person, small**: decide when a customer asks; build as an app on the platform where possible. |
