| I6-17 | security | **An address freed by an ended pod stays registered at the egress proxy until the sweep.** When a sandbox pod ends by itself (an exit, an eviction, a lost node), its address is free at once, but the Kubernetes driver unregisters it only when it sees the end (the sweep, every `sweep_every`; longer while the proxy cannot be reached). A new pod that gets the address in that window runs as the old app at the proxy, whatever tenant it belongs to: `ruum-egress` finds the identity by the source address and refuses the new pod's own registration (`subnet_overlap`).
