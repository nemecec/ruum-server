# Backlog 2026-11-12 (staging bring-up and the first sign-in test)

Staging (https://staging.ruum.space) runs main 3126d49 since 2026-11-12: Talos and Kubernetes on
three cx23 nodes in fsn1, the platform (CloudNativePG with backups to the locked Scaleway bucket,
SpiceDB, Zitadel, NATS, Mailpit), the edge with a ZeroSSL certificate, and ruum-server. All 16
smoke checks pass. On 2026-11-12 the person signed in with Google and with Microsoft through
Zitadel and landed in Ruum both times. These items came out of the bring-up and that test. Kinds,
efforts and handlings are as in `docs/backlog/2026-11-08.md`. All decisions here are
`provisional` (design 18.9).
