# Backlog 2026-11-08 (product loop, design 20)

The triage and product manager agent read the 13 retro files of the run of 2026-11-08
(`ruum-server-wt/panel/retro-*.md`: `copy`, `fu4`, `rev-a1`, `rev-k1`, `rq-1`, `rvs2a1`,
`rvs2q1`, `s2a1` (card and fix pass), `s2k1`, `s2q1` (card and fix pass), `secrev`, `secrev2`,
`step2-wave4-planner`, and the integrator's own `retro-integ.md`), and the reviews they cite.
Duplicates are merged; the "Seen by" column counts the retros that filed the item.

Each item has a kind (bug, feature, friction), evidence, a rough effort (S: under 2 hours of agent
time, M: half a day, L: more) and a proposed handling:

- **pipeline**: fix without a person (design 20.3: bugs with clear evidence, agent-experience
  fixes, reliability within the current scope). Goes into `docs/STATUS.md`, "Next cards".
- **panel**: an architect panel decision (agents, `provisional`), then the pipeline.
- **brief**: a person decides. The decision brief is in `docs/digests/2026-11-08.md`.
- **person, small**: the person's own machine or account; no decision needed.

All decisions here are `provisional` (design 18.9).
