# 0007: priorities after the TajuRuum survey

Status: decided by the person (staging), and provisional (the rest), 2026-11-11.

The survey of TajuRuum, the first concrete customer, shows what they need from Ruum (analysis by an
agent: a context pack for Claude and agents, plant and material catalogues, a work management app, an
address-based site data summary, watching planning changes, Dropbox).

1. **Step 1 goes live first** (decided by the person): staging starts now on Hetzner (`ruum-staging`
   project, budget about EUR 30-50 per month), then a first production when staging runs cleanly. The
   context pack (files over MCP) is useful to the customer already.
2. **Step 2, simple path first** (provisional): tables with no personal or secret columns (catalogues)
   are readable by agents and editable by people, solid and simple; the full Q-2 privacy work for
   personal data continues in parallel and does not block catalogues.
3. **Step 3 is the customer's value** (provisional): the work management app (Toggl Track, Toggl Plan,
   Asana in one) becomes the first project template ("internal tool with database and roles").
4. **Estonian public data as shared connections** (provisional): Maa- ja Ruumiamet WMS/WFS and 3D
   buildings, the planning registry, Riigi Teataja; reusable by every office; design during step 3
   (connection nodes 9.12, templates 2.7).
5. **Dropbox first among mounted drives** (provisional), ahead of Google Drive and OneDrive (7.4).
6. **Trust as a lead message** (provisional): EU hosting, no training on customer data, agents see
   stand-ins, everything logged; into the product direction draft.

Nothing is promised to the customer with a date (the survey analysis says the same).
